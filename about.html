Our Journey

Coffee King started as a single roaster in a small garage. What began as coffee for friends grew into the café you can visit today — same care, same standards, just more cups.

We roast in small batches, buy directly from growers, and pull every shot to order. No shortcuts, no automation replacing what a person should be doing by hand.
